Ambuyat

Ambuyat is brunei's national dish. It is made from sago starch and is mainly usually eaten with a variety of dipping sauces.

Nasi Katok

An affordable meal consisting of Chicken, Rice and Sambal. Price ranges from as low as $1.00 to $1.50.
