About Brunei

Historical facts, landscapes, weather, and more history.

'The Adobe of Peace'

Brunei Darussalam is a small country located on the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia. Despite its size, Brunei offers rich traditions, beautiful landscapes and modern attractions.

The Districts

Brunei is split into 4 districts, managed by district office under the MOHA(Ministry of Home Affairs).

History

Brunei has a rich history spanning over a thousand years, transitioning from an ancient trading port to a powerful regional empire, a British protectorate, and finally a modern, oil-rich Islamic sultanate.
