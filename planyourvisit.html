Everything you need to know before visiting Brunei Darussalam.

How do I go to Brunei?

Visitors can travel to Brunei by air, land or sea. Most international travellers arrive through Brunei International Airport. Visitors from nearby Malaysian states can also enter by land via the border. There are still ferry services but is rather limited due to the opening of the Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Bridge.

Getting Around

Dart is highly recommended for getting around long distances in Brunei. For short distances, walking is fine since Brunei streets are rather spacious or empty and is generally safe even at night.

Dart Official Website

Entry Requirements

Visa requirements depend on your nationality. Make sure your passport is valid and check the latest entry requirements before travelling.

Accommodation

Brunei offers different types of accommodation, from luxury hotels to affordable guesthouses. Visitors can choose a place to stay depending on their budget and travel preferences.

Best Time to Visit

Brunei has a tropical climate, meaning it is warm and humid throughout the year. February to April is generally a drier period, making it a good time for outdoor activities and sightseeing.
