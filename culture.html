Culture and Traditions

Traditions that date back from hundreds of years ago.

Traditional Clothing

Brunei's traditional clothing includes Baju Melayu for men and Baju Kurung for women. These outfits are commonly worn during celebrations and special occasions.

Eid al-Fitr

Eid al-Fitr, also commonly known as Hari Raya Aidilfitri, is one of Brunei's major celebrations, where families gather, visit relatives and enjoy traditional food.
