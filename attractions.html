Attractions to Visit in Brunei

Explore the 'Adobe of Peace' at its fullest

Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque

One of Brunei's most famous landmarks, known for its golden dome and beautiful architecture.

Kampung Ayer

A historic water village where visitors can experience traditional Bruneian lifestyle.

Ulu Temburong National Park

Discover Brunei's tropical rainforest, wildlife and beautiful natural scenery.

Jame' Asr Hassanil Bolkiah Mosque

A magnificent mosque featuring golden domes and impressive Islamic architecture.

Royal Regalia Museum

Learn about Brunei's royal history through its collections and historical exhibits.

The Empire Brunei

A luxury seaside resort known for its grand architecture and ocean views.

Jerudong Park

A popular recreational destination offering rides and entertainment for families.

Muara Beach

Arguably the best coastal destination with the most beautiful sunsets.

Istana Nurul Iman

The Residence of the Sultan of Brunei. Only open to public during Eid-al Fitr festivities
