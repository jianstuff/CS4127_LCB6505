Festivities in Brunei

Brunei celebrates various festivities throughout the year, but there are those that stands out the most.

Brunei's National Day

National Day is celebrated every 23rd February. Various performances and multiple governmental and private sectors march to demonstrate patriotism and loyalty to the country and His Majesty, the Sultan.

His Majesty's Birthday

Occurs in 15th July accompanied by a Grand Parade, 21-Gun Salute, Flag-Raising, Royal Airforce bypass and various food stalls opening at night for atleast a month.

Brunei's Half Marathon

Recent annual event where runners race on Brunei's Sultan Haji Omar 'Ali Saifuddien bridge that connects Brunei-Muara district to Temburong district over the sea spanning over 30km long.
