Visitor's Guide

Learn how to visit Brunei, and what's allowed and what isn't.

Respect Local Customs

Brunei is a Muslim-majority country, so visitors should respect local traditions and customs. As they say, if in Rome, do as the Romans do. This should be applied when visiting Brunei too.

Dress Appropriately

Wear modest and non-revealing clothing. This applies wherever you go in the country, especially in religious sites.

Weather and Climate

Brunei has a tropical climate with and is warm and extremely humid all year round. It is advisable to wear light clothing and bring sunscreen and sufficient water when going out.

Transportation

Public transports are scarce in Brunei compared to other countries. This is due to most of the population having personal cars. There are public buses on designated areas, but it is recommended to get 'Dart', which is basically Brunei's version of Grab or Uber.

Laws

As Brunei is a Muslim country, alcohol is highly restricted, which includes public consumption, buying and selling. Brunei also follows the Sharia Law, whcih should be followed. Of course, Brunei is lenient, so as long as these rules are not heavily breached, no issues will arise.

Language

The National language of Brunei is 'Bahasa Melayu'. Conversing in English is very plausable though as almost every single Bruneian citizen are fluent in English.
